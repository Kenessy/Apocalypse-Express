## Scene B3 — Greenhouse Supply Run

> [!summary] **SCENE B3 — Greenhouse Supply Run**  
> TYPE: Explore, Loot, Hazard  
> LENGTH: Short to Medium  
> DANGER: Risky  
> PURPOSE: Optional Prep  
> TEACHING: Light navigation / hazard reading  
>  
> **When this runs:** Optional route after **B2 — Chapel of the Second Dawn**, before the party heads to the **A-Tech Station**. The party learns about this place from a stalker-like survivor voice over radio while listening to broadcasts in the chapel.  
>  
> **Story job:**  
> - Give the players their first optional post-chapel choice before the main station route.  
> - Let them gather food, water, and basic supplies before the A-Tech train-station sequence.  
> - Show a frozen disaster image: a blast-wave or shockwave hit the greenhouse complex and threw glass through the air, where much of it froze mid-flight.  
> - Teach that the Halt preserves danger in impossible shapes, not just stillness.
>  
> **Player job:**  
> - Decide whether to spend time detouring before the Station.  
> - Navigate the accessible greenhouse lanes while avoiding suspended glass fields.  
> - Take what they can carry: food, usable plants, water containers, basic supplies, maybe simple tools.
>  
> **Tone:**  
> - Quiet, sunlit, and wrong: greenhouse warmth, dead irrigation, glittering frozen glass, old food life still clinging on.  
> - More survival-prep than major lore reveal. This is a small supply run before the larger A-Tech station problem.

---

### Block B3.1 — Radio Lead in the Chapel

> [!question] **TRIGGER — How the party learns about it**  
> - In B2, if the party listens through radio chatter / surviving broadcasts, they catch a rough survivor transmission.  
> - The speaker feels **stalker-like**: practical, half-feral, used to scavenging the broken world, and not interested in heroics.  
> - The broadcast points them toward nearby greenhouses as a supply opportunity before the Station.

> [!quote] **READ ALOUD — Radio fragment**  
> Static chews through the receiver, then a man’s voice slips in, close and tired. “If you’re north of the chapel and hungry, don’t waste time praying. The glasshouses still have food if you know where to step. Avoid the bright lanes. The glass is still in the air.”

> [!tip] **GM — Keep the lead simple**  
> - This is not a full NPC meeting unless you later want one. For now, the stalker-like figure is a radio voice.  
> - The point is to make the side route feel like discovered survivor knowledge, not a glowing quest marker.

---

### Block B3.2 — First View of the Greenhouses

> [!quote] **READ ALOUD — The broken glasshouses**  
> The greenhouses sit beyond the chapel grounds in a row of bent iron ribs and glinting panes. Something hit them hard — not a clean collapse, but a wave. The glass did not fall. It hangs in the air by the thousand, glittering at different heights like a storm that forgot to finish moving.

> [!abstract] **FEATURES — At a glance**  
> - Several connected greenhouse structures, most of them partly destroyed.  
> - A blast-wave / shockwave pattern: panes blown inward and outward, metal frames bent, tables overturned.  
> - Large sections are **not walkable** because suspended glass shards fill the air at body height.  
> - A few safer lanes remain where the glass either fell, never broke, or froze high enough to duck under.  
> - Usable food/supplies are visible beyond the dangerous sections.

---

### Block B3.3 — Navigating the Suspended Glass

> [!warning] **RULE — Hazard, not combat**  
> - No enemies required. The danger is environmental: frozen glass, bad footing, and greed.  
> - Consequences should usually be cuts, torn clothing, lost time, or Minor Injuries, not lethal damage.

> [!abstract] **CHECKS — Suggested gates**  
> Use these as defaults if the party pushes into risky lanes.  
>  
> **Read the safe path:** AWS / Awareness vs DC 12.  
> - Success: identify one safe lane and one obvious no-go area.  
> - Crit: also spot a richer supply pocket with tolerable risk.  
>  
> **Move through glass lanes:** RES or finesse-equivalent vs DC 13.  
> - Success: cross safely.  
> - Failure: torn gear, dropped item, or superficial cuts.  
> - Critical failure: 1 Minor Injury from slicing glass or a bad stumble.  
>  
> **Extract supplies from awkward shelves:** PRY / SYS / appropriate tool check vs DC 12.  
> - Success: recover supplies cleanly.  
> - Failure: supplies still recoverable, but cost time or make noise.  
> - Critical success: recover extra preserved goods.

> [!tip] **GM — PAVNN use**  
> - PAVNN can carry crates and mark safe lanes, but should not solve the whole scene alone.  
> - If the party stalls, PAVNN can identify that the glass pattern came from a pressure wave, not ordinary decay.

---

### Block B3.4 — Loot Profile

> [!abstract] **LOOT — What this place is for**  
> The greenhouse is a **pre-station supply stop**, not a major revelation node. Keep rewards practical.  
>  
> Possible finds:  
> - Preserved fruit / vegetables / edible greenhouse produce.  
> - Sealed water or irrigation containers.  
> - Basic food bundles for the next leg.  
> - Gardening knives, twine, plastic sheeting, hand tools.  
> - Small first-aid scraps from a worker station, if the party needs a soft heal.  
> - A cracked field radio battery or spare part, if you want a minor utility pickup.

> [!tip] **GM — Carry limit**  
> - Let them take what they can plausibly carry.  
> - This can feed into inventory / load limits later, but do not turn this into a bookkeeping slog.

---

### Block B3.5 — Exit Toward the Station

> [!success] **OUTCOME — If the party completes the side route**  
> - The party gains food and basic supplies before the A-Tech Station.  
> - They have seen their first frozen environmental hazard outside the chapel.  
> - They understand that the Halt can preserve violence mid-motion, not merely freeze peaceful things.  
> - They are now better prepared to continue toward the **A-Tech Station** and the **Offboarder auto-turret** set-piece.

> [!failure] **If they skip it**  
> Nothing breaks. This route is optional. They simply reach the Station with fewer supplies and less practice reading Halt hazards.
