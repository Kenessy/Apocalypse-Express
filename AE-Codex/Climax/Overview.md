## Stage and players

**Stage:** the broken-bridge / mountaintop "end of the world" location. *[T2: A5 Sundown Saloon — Lucifer's "where the world comes apart"]*

**Lucifer's presence at the stage:** his **avatar** (per [`Characters/Lucifer.md → presence modes`](../Characters/Lucifer.md#the-three-presence-modes--locked)). The avatar **cannot teleport**, which is why the players must bring the Orb to him — **his location is fixed**.

**The Orb on stage:** **already there**, mounted in the Apocalypse Express locomotive since Chapter 1 ("The Gauntlet"). The party brings the train to the stage; the Orb is on board. *[T1: Daniel + T2: Obsidian/Canto 1]*

**Cicero on stage:** present, with quill and book. He has known this moment is coming since reading the book. He has a plan. *[T1: Cicero awareness lock]*

**PAVNN on stage:** present. Sulfur accumulator carried for the post-defiance phase. *[T2: Workshop/AICore/PAVNN — to be re-verified]*

**The AI Core:** still resident in the train (its first-carriage origin). *[T1: AI Core lock]*

## The three endings — locked

The campaign exhausts three orthogonal paths. Player choice (or default-by-inaction) determines which:

### Humanity path — defiance

Players resist Lucifer. Lucifer takes/uses the Orb himself. The Orb drains him (no, it drains everyone — but Lucifer is otherworldly so the drain pulls his Hell-energy through), the **Eye now sees Lucifer**, the **divine-ignore pact breaks**, the seal cracks, the new hellfire activates. Lucifer's avatar self-immolates by choice (this body is disposable now).

**Then** — and here Cicero strikes the avatar with the quill, dies in the act, and instructs the party to descend into Hell to finish where it started. The party uses the locomotive (stripped of carriages, fueled with collected hellfire) to rocket through the breached portal into Hell.

**In Hell:** PAVNN's sulfur accumulator nullifies Lucifer's protective spell. The quill, in otherworld physics, becomes a **sword** (form follows need). Final boss fight against Lucifer's true form. Players win → continue ending / soft restart.

**Outcome:** the world survives (damaged, scarred). New hellfire spread arrested. Cycle interrupted. *[T1: structurally locked across multiple sessions]*

### Robot path — AI Core ascendance

Players support the AI Core. AI carries its brain core to the broadcast position; the Orb is used to project a **gamma broadcast** that upgrades all robots to move through frozen time. Side effect: gamma destroys all organic life, including the players. AI then triggers the atomic bomb (carried up from Chapter 9 "Siege of DarkHold") to **erase the site**, ensuring no reversal.

**Outcome:** limbo / robot inheritance. Frozen time persists. Robots become the new dominant form. The cosmic problem is sidestepped, not solved. *[T1: structurally locked]*

### Lucifer path — compliance / collaboration

Players hand over the Orb. Or remain passive while Lucifer takes it. Lucifer activates as in defiance, but uninterrupted. The Eye sees him; the seal breaks; the new hellfire spreads; Cicero records faithfully and dies in the firestorm with the world; Lucifer takes the manuscript and the Orb to Hell.

**Outcome:** hard restart / new universe. The current universe ends. Lucifer is the architect of the next one (using Cicero's manuscript as source-document). *[T1: structurally locked]*

## Why the Orb activation is the climax-axis

The Orb activation is **the moment that breaks the divine-ignore pact** (per [`Cosmology/DivineIgnore.md`](../Cosmology/DivineIgnore.md)). The pupil-aperture turns inward on Lucifer's hand → God's eye sees Lucifer for the first time in the campaign's timescale → divine attention floods back.

**But by Lucifer's design, this attention arrives too late** — the seal is already cracking, the new hellfire (years-soaked into the substrate via train operation) is already spreading, the manuscript is in his hand. God's belated look catches the apocalypse mid-bloom, not pre-launch.

This **is the gambit**: orchestrate the moment when the snap-back of divine attention becomes the seal-break, not its prevention.
