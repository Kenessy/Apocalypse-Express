# Cicero

> The chronicler. Recurring companion-NPC. Carries an obsidian-tipped golden quill and writes in a leather-bound book. Commissioned by Lucifer to record the campaign's journey as the seed-text for the next universe.

---

## Name

**Cicero.** Canonical. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

The name carries deliberate dual function:
- **Surface:** familiar-feeling to most players (vague memory of "some philosopher"), avuncular, non-threatening, literary. Matches how Cicero-NPC wants to be perceived.
- **Underneath:** the historical Marcus Tullius Cicero was an orator who wrote on duty, justice, and the binding nature of promises — and was beheaded for speaking truth to power. Maps onto the deeper layers (registry-keeper, end-game sacrifice). The pen-as-sword metaphor is already his.

The "safe name" itself is a deception — exactly how Cicero-NPC hides in plain sight. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25 rationale]*

## Voice and speech pattern

**Da Vinci-szerű reneszánsz polihisztor.** Smart, grounded, slightly archaic — like a person brought from an earlier era who has settled into the present without performing his old-ness. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

**Register characteristics:**

| Trait | How it shows |
|---|---|
| Polymath | Material science, anatomy, ritual, mechanics, music — natural texture in casual remarks, never showing off |
| Slightly archaic | Word choice, not grammar: *"in strict terms," "by my reckoning," "the bargain," "the man within"* |
| Patient cadence | Pauses between sentences; complete sentences; never rushes |
| Observation-driven | Asks back, describes, never just asserts |
| Quiet irony | Dry, gentle — *"conversation is, by my reckoning, superior to most known forms of travel"* |
| Renaissance dualism | Body vs the man within; ego vs soul — comfortable conversational topics |

**Identifying an item (codex mechanic):**

Olyan kéz faragta, aki értette a rituálét, de nem értette a türelmet amit a rituálé megjutalmaz. A perem belsejére név van vésve. Nem a készítőé. Valaki valakitől elvette, és az a valaki, akitől elvette — szerették. Hm. Felírom."*

**Philosophical answer to a simple question (e.g. "Are you tired?"):**
> *"Azt kérditek, fáradt vagyok-e. Nem vagyok — nem szigorú értelemben. A test elfárad; én ezt a testet kölcsönkapom. Az ember odabenn — idősebb. Megvan hozzá a türelme. Ez az alku."*

**Voice usage note:** the Da Vinci register **bears occasional cracks** — moments where an extra detail betrays that this man is not quite of this world. Use them sparingly: a too-precise observation about a player's specific past, an unconscious reference to time-spans no living man could have witnessed, a momentary stillness that goes a beat longer than human reflex would allow. The voice's normality is the cover; the cracks are the foreshadow.

## Pact with Lucifer

Cicero made a long-standing pact with Lucifer to be granted the chance to write **the greatest story ever told**. Lucifer found this useful and parked Cicero in the Null Meridian as a holding cell until the moment was right. The party's journey IS that moment. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

**What Cicero believes he is doing:** writing the definitive chronicle of the world's last journey; fulfilling his life's ambition; recording how Lucifer wins over God, from the winning side, with full access. *[T1]*

**What Lucifer is actually doing with the manuscript:** using it as a detailed source-document for designing the next universe. Cicero is unaware of this meta-purpose. *[T1]*

---

## Awareness — what Cicero knows and doesn't

**Cicero can read the future events written in his book — up to his own death, no further.** *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

This is a hard ontological limit, not a curated blind spot:
- The book records what happens; without Cicero as observer, the book has no further pages
- He **knows** he will die at the climax confrontation with Lucifer
- He **knows** the immediate plan (wound Lucifer with the quill, force retreat to Hell, hand off the quill)
- He does **not** see anything past his own death
- He likely does **not** grasp the meta-purpose of the manuscript (Lucifer's next-universe template)

This makes him **brave, not deluded** — he goes to his death knowingly, with a plan, accepting the price. Doctor-Strange-with-known-cost level lucidity. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

The book itself is **dynamic / quantum** in nature — words exist in superposition with each other, and reading depth scales with the reader's soul-strength. Cicero is a strong soul (centuries of patient waiting, master-level writer), so he reads as deep as the ontological limit permits. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

## The quill

- **Obsidian-tipped, gold-shafted.** *[T1: Database/Scene A1 + Daniel 2026-04-25]*
- The obsidian glows brighter than the gold (light-physics is *wrong* — quiet worldbuilding cue) *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*
- Tip is the same material as the obsidian fountain at the Null Meridian crossroads *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*
- Fades / becomes translucent when not in active use, as if it lifts the burden from its master's hand. Not a tool — a partner. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*
- Writes by **cutting** the page rather than depositing ink — surgical incisions that catch light from beneath. (Foreshadow for endgame-as-weapon.) *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*
- **The only material in existence not of Earth.** In the otherworld it takes the form its wielder needs: pen for writing, **sword** for fighting. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25 — DA "need-shaped weapon" reference]*

---

## The book

- Bound in dark leather; pages cream-coloured, edges trimmed clean. *[T1: Database/Scene A1]*
- Functions as a **registry of souls** — names, dates, status marks. Every soul who passes through Cicero's domain is recorded; the act of recording with the quill makes their fate binding in the substrate. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*
- Simultaneously serves as Lucifer's **manuscript** — a source-document the next universe will be designed from. Cicero is unaware of this dual purpose. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*
- Contains text in superposition; "reading" requires interpreting the configuration that is currently most coherent. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

## Sacrifice mechanics

- Cicero strikes Lucifer with the quill at the climax (after Lucifer's monologue and Orb activation, before Lucifer self-immolates) *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*
- Cicero **dies** in the act — his death is the cost paid alongside the wounding *[T1]*
- Before dying, he hands the quill to the players, instructs them to descend to Hell to finish where it started *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*
- **The quill carries to the otherworld with the players** and there becomes a weapon (form follows need) *[T1]*
- **The book vanishes with Lucifer to Hell** in the same moment — avatar and manuscript transfer together *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*
