# Cosmology — The Substrate

> What the world is fundamentally made of and how its forces relate. Built up from verified facts only.

---

## Hellfire

**Hellfire burns souls.** [T1: Database/Rule 6 Currencies]

> *"Hellfire burns souls. Sparkplugs can be charged by what lingers after death, pressed into the canister's repulsive field until the pocket stabilizes. The result is portable, obedient energy — distilled from what used to be someone."*

**Sparkplugs** are canisters of soul-derived energy. They are rare, taboo, beyond price. They power Hellfire boilers, Revival prints, weapons. *[T1: Database/Rule 6]*

**One canister will wake a Hellfire boiler and shove a steam train through purple haze.** *[T1: Database/Rule 6]*

The Apocalypse Express runs on a Hellfire boiler. *[T1: implied across Rule 1, Rule 6, A3]*

---

## The new hellfire

A **developed/upgraded variant of hellfire ash** that Lucifer experimentally created. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

Differences from standard hellfire:
- Doesn't just **purify** souls (transition) — actively **burns / consumes** them (terminal erasure, no continuation) *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*
- **Higher energy yield** *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*
- **Can be brought up to the real world** — standard hellfire couldn't survive the cross-realm transit *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*
- Powers the Apocalypse Express specifically. The AE is **the first prototype to survive the HALT transit** running on this fuel. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

The players unwittingly use the new hellfire **throughout the campaign** by virtue of riding the train.

## The Pause

Earth is suspended mid-double-apocalypse by **Lucifer's chrono machine**. *[T1: Database/Scene B1 + Daniel 2026-04-25]* See [PausedApocalypse.md](PausedApocalypse.md).

## Trueflow (anti-pause)

The **World Anchor** projects a **Trueflow field** — a celestial anti-time projection that resists Lucifer's chrono machine pause and unfreezes the world locally. *[T2: Obsidian/Canto 7 v0.1]*

In Trueflow radius:
- Time-skew effects do not apply
- Legacy ordnance (frozen mid-flight nukes etc.) **thaw on re-entry to Trueflow**
- This is the divine side's tool to complete the apocalypse against Lucifer's pause

Players neutralise the Trueflow Anchor in Canto VII to allow the AE to pass. *[T2: Obsidian/Canto 7 v0.1]*
