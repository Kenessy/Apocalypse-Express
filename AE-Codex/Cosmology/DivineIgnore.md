# Cosmology — The Divine-Ignore Pact

> God, exhausted by Lucifer's persistent provocations, decided to **filter Lucifer out** entirely — to cease attending to him at all. The pact is unilateral on God's side, but operationally it gives Lucifer **the loophole he has been exploiting ever since**: he can act anywhere on Earth, in plain sight, because the only being who could stop him *isn't looking*. The whole Apocalypse Express setup runs in this divine blind spot.

---

## The mechanism

**Cause:** Lucifer kept annoying God with provocations, contradictions, manipulations — *"sokszor bosszantotta"*. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

**God's response:** Decision to **filter Lucifer out**. To stop attending to him. To **ignore** him entirely, treating him as a perpetual prankster not worth divine attention. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

**Result:** Lucifer becomes **invisible to God's awareness**. Not invisible to the world — visible to other beings, to humans, to angels — but **God Himself does not perceive him**.

**Lucifer's exploit:** He uses this divine blind spot to operate **freely on Earth**. The only force that could stop him at the cosmic level **does not see him**. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

---

## Why this is the foundational mechanism

The divine-ignore pact is **not a peripheral lore detail**. It is the **operating system on which Lucifer's entire campaign runs**.

| Without the pact | With the pact |
|---|---|
| Lucifer cannot operate on Earth at all (God would intervene) | Lucifer can do anything on Earth that doesn't FORCE God to look |
| Cicero's pact is impossible (God would notice) | Cicero is invisible to divine attention while contracted to Lucifer |
| The Apocalypse Express cannot run (it's running on new hellfire, which God would object to) | The train is in the blind spot |
| The chrono machine pause cannot be applied (God would unfreeze immediately) | Lucifer's pause holds because God isn't watching |
| The whole campaign cannot exist | The whole campaign is what one entity gets up to in the divine blind spot |

---

## How this connects to Overreach's "never get caught cheating"

Lucifer **must not be caught lying** because **getting caught = forcing God to look = breaking the ignore-pact**. The cardinal rule of [Overreach](Overreach.md) is operationally identical to "stay in the divine blind spot."

This is why his clever-wording is so meticulous:
- A **direct lie** would be a discoverable deception. If exposed, the divine attention snaps back, the ignore-pact is voided.
- **Clever wording** is technically true. Even under audit, no one can call it a lie. The blind spot holds.

---

## Why God turned His back on Earth (and the Eye)

When God decided to ignore Lucifer, he ALSO **turned away from Earth itself** — because Earth is where Lucifer would inevitably make trouble, and ignoring Lucifer required not-looking at the place where Lucifer would surface.

**He took most of Himself away. But His eye stayed.**

The Eye of God = the [Orb](../Items/Orb.md). Left behind in the substrate when He averted His attention. He cannot easily retrieve it without re-attending to Earth (which would mean re-attending to Lucifer too). So it stays — anchored in the substrate, drained but not dispatched, waiting.

The fanatics who say *"Isten szeme még mindig itt van"* are **technically correct** — they think they're being metaphorical, but the eye is literally here. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

## How this re-explains the double apocalypse

(See [`PausedApocalypse.md`](PausedApocalypse.md) for the canonical history.)

The divine-ignore pact gives the apocalypse new mechanical clarity:

- **Atomic apocalypse trigger:** humans — Lucifer's "spark" beings — eventually shouted loud enough by destroying themselves that even an averted ear could hear them. The missiles flew.
- **Divine response:** God turned His head **back** for a moment, just enough to register the noise. Angels, trumpets, demons, rapture — His **belated, half-attentive** answer. *Late*, because He had been pointedly not-looking.
- **Lucifer's pause:** At the exact moment God's attention snapped back, Lucifer activated the chrono machine — **catching God mid-look, freezing the moment of partial-attention**. Now God is **stuck in a glance** He doesn't want to be having. The ignore-pact has been forcibly partially-broken by humanity's own actions, and Lucifer holds the pause to prevent the divine attention from completing its motion.

The "strain in the bones" *[Database/B1]* is **God's averted attention straining against the pause** — He wants to look away again, but Lucifer holds Him in the partial-glance.

---

## How this re-explains the climax

When Lucifer holds the Orb at the climax, the pupil's gaze turns **inward, on him**. **He is now LOOKING at God's eye, and God's eye is now AT HIM.** The ignore-pact is broken not by humans this time — by Lucifer's direct manipulation of the Eye itself.

But Lucifer **engineered this moment** for it to happen:
- The new hellfire is already spreading through the substrate (decades of train operation = years of substrate-soaking)
- Cicero's manuscript is at his side — the seed-document for the next universe
- The Orb is in his hand — God's word about to override God's seal
- The avatar is positioned to be discarded — exit ramp ready

The divine attention floods back, **but it floods into the apocalypse's continuation, not its prevention**. God's "look" arrives with all the hellfire, all the souls, all the manuscript. **It is too late to stop. It is also too late to ignore.** Lucifer wins by forcing the attention at the worst possible moment for God, the best possible moment for himself.

## Implications for player agency

The players are **also in the blind spot** while contracted to Lucifer (post-A5 pact). This means:
- They are **invisible to divine attention** while inside the Lucifer-system
- They could potentially **break out** of the blind spot by performing some action that forces God to attend to them
- But doing so might **trigger the same attention-snap** that Lucifer is engineering for the climax — a misuse of the loophole

## Implications for the Trueflow / World Anchor

The **World Anchor** (Canto 7) projects Trueflow — a celestial counter-pause. This is plausibly the **mechanism by which God-attention can locally re-enter Earth** without His full consent — angels using the Anchor to project His residual presence into specific zones.

**Players neutralising the Anchor in Canto 7 = preventing local God-attention from waking** = **keeping Lucifer's blind-spot operation safe for the climax**. The party are unwittingly maintaining the divine-ignore pact's integrity for Lucifer's benefit.
