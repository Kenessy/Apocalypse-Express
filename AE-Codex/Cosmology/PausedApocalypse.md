# Cosmology — The Paused Apocalypse

> What halted the world, and how the pause holds.

---

## The double apocalypse

Earth is in a **paused double apocalypse**. *[T1: Database/Scene B1, Lucifer's monologue]*

> *"You remember the headlines. Wars, little plagues, big egos. Eventually someone pushed the wrong stack of buttons and lit the sky. Your upstairs neighbour responded with angels and trumpets. Double exposure: man-made suicide, divine eviction."* *[T1: Database/Scene B1]*

**Layer 1 — Atomic apocalypse (man-made):** Humanity launched their nuclear arsenal — global, unrecoverable. *[T1: Database/Scene B1]*

**Layer 2 — Divine apocalypse:** As the missiles flew, God responded with angels descending, trumpets sounding, sky tearing, demons emerging from cracks in the earth, rapture taking souls upward. *[T1: Database/Scene B1, Scene B2 worker's-last-moments flash]*

The two apocalypses **overlap rather than sequence** — the missiles never detonated and the divine intervention never completed because of the next event.

---

## Lucifer's intervention (the pause)

> *"I did what I could. I stepped out onto the film and pressed pause. It didn't stop cleanly. Some places are frozen mid-scream; others judder forward in fits and starts. That strain you feel in your bones? That's the world trying to finish dying."* *[T1: Database/Scene B1]*

**Lucifer stepped onto the fabric of reality and activated the chrono machine.** *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25 (clarifying canon)]*

**The chrono machine** is Lucifer's device. It paused time globally, but **did not stop cleanly**. The pause requires Lucifer's continuous effort to maintain. *[T1: Database/Scene B1 + Daniel 2026-04-25]*

## The current world-state

Most of the world is frozen mid-cataclysm — a still frame of:
- Mushroom clouds half-bloomed, fallout particles caught mid-fall
- Lava mid-pour from cracks
- Demons half-emerged, mouths open in soundless roar
- Angels mid-descent
- Rapture-people suspended halfway between earth and sky
- Trumpets blasting silently

Above all of it: a **purple chronon haze** in uneven veils. *[T1: Database/Sora visual guide]*

The **Apocalypse Express train and its steam/smoke are the only things moving freely** in the paused world. *[T1: Database/Sora visual guide]*

---

## The Drift

Some zones are **not perfectly frozen** — they "judder forward in fits and starts" *[T1: Database/Scene B1]*. These zones are called **The Drift**, and beings within them are called **Drifters**. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25 — naming from brainstorm canvas, confirmed]*

Drift zones are where:
- Threats can advance (slowly)
- Time leaks forward in bursts
- The seal is thinnest

## Why the train can move

The Apocalypse Express runs on **the new hellfire** (see [Substrate.md → "the new hellfire"](Substrate.md#the-new-hellfire)) — the only fuel that survives cross-realm transit (HALT) and operates in the real world. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-25]*

The players, having Revival-printed bodies after the A5 pact, share in the train's exemption from the freeze. They are essentially **proxies of the train's own animation**. *[T2: derived from Database/Rule 1 Revival + Sora guide; needs explicit lock]*

## The "strain in the bones"

> *"That strain you feel in your bones? That's the world trying to finish dying."* *[T1: Database/Scene B1]*

A continuous low-level sensory cue felt by all conscious beings during the pause. The world is straining to complete its cataclysm — Lucifer's chrono machine holds it back at constant cost.

---

## Counter-force — Trueflow

The celestial side projects **Trueflow** (see [Substrate.md → "Trueflow"](Substrate.md#trueflow-anti-pause)) — a counter-projection that locally cancels the pause. The **World Anchor** is its source.

Where Trueflow is active:
- Time advances normally
- Frozen ordnance thaws (legacy nukes resume their flight)
- The two apocalypses can complete

The campaign's central conflict in part is: keep Lucifer's pause holding (preserve agency) vs the celestial drive to let the divine apocalypse complete.
