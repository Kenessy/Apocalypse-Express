## CORE — story-blocking

### Q1 — What word names what Lucifer planted in humanity? *(re-asked clearly 2026-04-25; word DEFERRED 2026-04-25)*

> [!important] **Word deferred by Daniel: "nemtom még mi azt egyelőre napoljuk"**. Constraints below remain valid for when we revisit. **Surrounding cosmology has been locked** — see Cosmology/Substrate.md "The Spark — divine source of life".



**Prompt:** In Daniel's brainstorm Lucifer monologue draft, this exact phrasing appears:

> *"I gave him **the spark** to create life, human life — the ember that fans the flame. The single idea of rebellion, of standing up against their own creator, the idea of 'being better than what came before' — in one word: '**xy — we need something that fits here**'."*

The word "spark" here is **casual English in the draft**, NOT a defined term. The placeholder **`xy`** is where Daniel wanted **one specific word** that names what Lucifer's gift IS.

**Why it matters:** This is the cosmological touchstone — names what Lucifer planted in humanity, what hellfire physically manifests, what the climax burns away or honors. The monologue is incomplete without it. Without it, the entire Cosmology/Substrate cannot finalise.

**Constraints:** Single word ideally. Captures: rebellion + ambition + creation + free will + transcending the creator. Fits AE tonality (industrial-arcane, gold-and-obsidian, infernal-sacred).

**Confirmed NOT the answer:** Hellstatic (clarified by Daniel — that's a death-residue mechanic).

**Open design choice — connect to Sparkplug or stay independent?**
- **(α) Tie to Sparkplug mechanic.** Sparkplugs are *"distilled from what used to be someone"* — i.e., soul-residue energy. If Lucifer's gift IS the soul's rebellion-capacity, then every Sparkplug is a fragment of his gift. Strong worldbuilding integration. Word options: capitalised "the Spark", "the Plug", "Sparkfire", direct rhymes.
- **(β) Independent word.** Doesn't tie to Sparkplug. Word options: Embercreed, the Climb, the Reach, Defiance, Becoming, Hellscale, Heatfire, Brightfall, etc.

Daniel's pick for direction (α / β) determines the candidate space. Then the actual word.

### Q25 — How does combat work in the HTML VN format? (NEW, opened 2026-04-26)

**Prompt:** D&D combat ruleset is the planned-D&D-port version, but the immediate VN format (HTML-based, click trees, dice rolls) needs its own combat resolution. Daniel: *"terveztem combatot dnd be bár visual novelbe no idea mit tegyünk és hogy."*

**Open design space:**
- Click-driven choice resolution with dice modifiers (Disco Elysium thoughts/skills style)
- Round-based: enemy → choice → dice → outcome → enemy → ...
- Single-roll abstracted resolution (one big roll determines the fight)
- No combat (resolve via skill checks / narrative escape options)
- Card-based / sequence-based
- Body-tag-gated combat options (different tags get different action menus)

**Why it matters:** Combat scenes need a chosen mechanic before any encounter is written. Without this, combat-relevant character / NPC / item docs (e.g., PAVNN's "doesn't excel at frontline combat" descriptor, the quill-as-sword in Hell, the CRAM Carriage) lack a target system.

**Possible agent-research path:** if Daniel wants, agents can survey how Citizen Sleeper / Disco Elysium / Pathologic / Tales-of-the-Aria / web-based dice-VN games handle combat — to find the right inspiration before locking the AE approach.

**Priority:** IMPORTANT.

- **They don't see** → The players see Cicero / PAVNN looking at a mirror that contains Lucifer playing chess "against" them — a haunting tableau. Cicero / PAVNN are unwitting subjects of Lucifer's amusement, not partners.

**Priority:** IMPORTANT — affects how the chess-scenes are written and what Cicero / PAVNN's awareness levels are.

### Q16 — 9th rank: exclusive (race) or shared (coalition)? (NEW, opened 2026-04-25)

**Prompt:** Reaching the 9th rank = escaping the board, becoming a player. **Is this exclusive (only one being can ascend per game) or shared (multiple beings can ascend together)?**

If exclusive → climax becomes a race between Lucifer and the party. Three endings now have a 4th sibling: "the party reaches it first."

**Why it matters:** Determines whether the climax is a race or a negotiation. Affects the framing of every ending.

**Priority:** IMPORTANT — affects climax architecture revisit (Q4).
