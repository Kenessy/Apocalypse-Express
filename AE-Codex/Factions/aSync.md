# aSync

> Pre-Pause experimental tech subsidiary that focused on **time-asynchronous systems** — algorithms, hardware, and AI built to operate outside conventional time-synchronization. The name is literal: **a-sync = asynchronous**. A-Tech absorbed them pre-Pause; the legacy distinction lives on for what each unit specialized in. Their unfinished super-smart AI mainframe is what Lucifer scavenged and mounted on the Apocalypse Express as the AI Core.

## The "asynchronous" specialty — and why it matters

The corp's name is the literal description of their work: **systems that operate out of time-sync**. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-26 — explicit clarification]*

This is structurally enormous in the campaign cosmology:

- The **Pause** (per [`../Cosmology/PausedApocalypse.md`](../Cosmology/PausedApocalypse.md)) freezes most conventional technology because standard engines and systems are time-bound (diesel needs combustion timing, electric needs grid sync, etc. — see [`../Items/ApocalypseExpress.md → "Why hellfire is the only fuel that works"`](../Items/ApocalypseExpress.md#why-hellfire-is-the-only-fuel-that-works)).
- aSync's tech, by design, was **already built to operate outside synchronized time**. When the Pause hit, their R&D systems were **uniquely positioned to keep functioning** in the frozen world.
- This is **plausibly why Lucifer found their abandoned AI Core useful**: a regular AI mainframe wouldn't run in the Pause. An aSync AI was designed for asynchronous operation — it fits the timestop-resistance regime that hellfire provides for the train.

> [!important]
> aSync's name and specialty is **not coincidental coloring**. It is the engineering reason their stolen tech works inside Lucifer's apparatus. The Pause is an asynchronous environment; aSync built for asynchronous environments.

## Why aSync built the AI Core

A top-secret R&D direction inside aSync was building a super-intelligent AI mainframe with the goal of **replacing the corrupt leadership** (presumably aSync's own / its parent A-Tech's leadership). *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-26 — already locked in AICore.md]*

This was an internal coup-attempt project. **Alignment problems** prevented it from being completed. The unfinished core sat dormant somewhere when the Pause hit. Lucifer found it and grabbed it.

The choice of an **aSync** team to build this was probably deliberate — if you're going to build an AI to replace the leaders who control time-sensitive systems, you'd want the team that knows how to work outside time-sync constraints.
