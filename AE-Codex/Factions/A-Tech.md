# A-Tech

> A pre-Pause megacorp on the scale of Sombra Corp from Overwatch. Mass-produces military hardware including most of the world's robotics. Absorbed the smaller experimental aSync corporation pre-Pause; from that point on the two names refer to the same conglomerate, with the legacy distinction preserved only for what each unit originally specialized in.

## Acquired aSync (pre-Pause)

A-Tech absorbed the smaller experimental **aSync** corporation at some point before the Pause. *[T1: Daniel 2026-04-26]*

After the absorption:
- The two names are technically interchangeable (both are A-Tech now)
- The **legacy distinction** is preserved for **specialty**:
  - A-Tech-original = mass robotics, military hardware
  - aSync-legacy = experimental AI, time-asynchronous tech (the "a-sync" in the name)
- Personnel and projects from aSync continued to operate inside A-Tech, sometimes still called by the old aSync name

This is why Tier 1 in-world sources can label things as **A-Tech** or **aSync** depending on which legacy unit produced them. Both correct.

## A-Tech as faction in Canto 1

In **Canto 1 (The Gauntlet)**, A-Tech operates as a **hostile force** alongside (post-absorption: same entity as) aSync around the Tunnels/Temple where the Orb is acquired. They are **retrieve-or-deny operating** — trying to get the Orb themselves or prevent anyone else from getting it. *[T2: Obsidian/Canto 1 v1.7]*

Whether they understand WHAT the Orb is (= the Eye of God, per [`../Items/Orb.md`](../Items/Orb.md)) or are operating on partial / wrong information is **open**.

## A-Tech ↔ Lucifer

A-Tech as an ENTITY is not (so far) known to be in any kind of pact with Lucifer. Lucifer has, however, **opportunistically grabbed A-Tech property** for his own apparatus:

- **The AI Core** — abandoned aSync (now A-Tech) R&D project; Lucifer found it in unfinished state, mounted it on the Apocalypse Express. See [`../Characters/AICore.md`](../Characters/AICore.md).
- **PAVNN** — A-Tech-built mule android, dormant in the chapel basement; Lucifer woke him with a finger-snap (bypassing the activation-phrase requirement). See [`../Characters/PAVNN.md`](../Characters/PAVNN.md).

In both cases, Lucifer didn't commission these — he **scavenged** them. The corp continues to operate independently (hostile in Canto 1) without knowing its lost properties are now Lucifer's tools.
