Morning, noon, evening — twilight by day, darkness by night... An infinite loop. But what is a loop? A circuit where we wake up, go to work, survive, then fall asleep. Every day, around and around — like a hamster in a wheel. Neuron A, B, C — back to A again, forever repeating the same cycle.

A circuit where current builds until it breaches a threshold. In the brain, a synapse... the signal arcs and the thought spirals, seeking its desired effect. And we organise our entire lives around it.

But what is the purpose? Of the loop? To keep something — us — in the loop. As long as necessary. So what is the loop? A stable loop of days and nights, suffering and love, hate and compassion... We naturally gravitate toward this. Because we know the rule: in a stable loop... structure emerges. We need that structure so desperately that we cling even to the familiarity of our own suffering.

Am I on the right path?

Evening follows morning, morning follows evening. What is it that breaks us free? What if we step sideways, out of the loop? Anything that breaks the logic is enough. It is enough to wake in the dark.

It is enough to just wake. So — let us wake.
