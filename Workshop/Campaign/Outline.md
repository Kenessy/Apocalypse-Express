# Campaign Outline — 10-Chapter Structure

> [!summary]
> The campaign is structured as **10 chapters** (titles brainstormed in `Assets/Canvas/(Canvas) Ideas brainstorming.canvas`). Chapter 1 is the Prelude (A1-A5 + B1-B2, currently in `Database/40_Scenes/`). Chapter 9 is **Siege of DarkHold** — the atomic bomb origin map for the climax. Chapter 10 is **Gambit for the 9th rank** — the climax we have fully designed in `Climax/`. Chapters 2-8 are the **B-arc body** (the Orb retrieval journey) and have varying levels of design completion.

## What this anchors

### Chapter 9 — Siege of DarkHold

The atomic bomb that gets mounted on the Apocalypse Express in the climax (per `Climax/OVERVIEW.md → Phase 2`) **originates here**. Whatever the DarkHold is (a fortress? an enemy stronghold? a research site?), the players besiege it and acquire the bomb.

**Implications:**
- The DarkHold is on Earth (or at least reachable by the train)
- Whoever holds it is significant enough to warrant a siege
- The atomic bomb is the trophy — used in Chapter 10 either as the AI's site-erasure tool (robot path) or as a red herring against Lucifer (humanity path)

> [!todo] Worldbuilding for the DarkHold
> Who holds it? What is inside? Why a "siege" — does it require multi-session, full-arc effort?
> What other prizes / lore drops / NPCs does this chapter offer beyond the bomb?

### Chapter 10 — Gambit for the 9th rank

This is the climax we fully designed. The title — **"the 9th rank"** — is new worldbuilding context.

**Likely meaning:** the Pseudo-Dionysian celestial hierarchy has 9 orders of angels (Seraphim, Cherubim, Thrones, Dominions, Virtues, Powers, Principalities, Archangels, Angels — though "9th rank" could mean either the highest or lowest depending on counting direction). Lucifer's monologue ends with: *"egyenesen a te székedbe? És meglássuk hogy néz ki a világ az ÉN URALMAM ALATT?"* — claiming God's seat. The "9th rank" likely refers to either:

- **The highest angelic rank** (Seraphim) — Lucifer's claim to the topmost throne
- **God's own position** — sometimes counted as a 9th tier above the angelic orders
- **A specific cosmological rank** unique to AE's worldbuilding

> [!todo] Lock the meaning of "9th rank"
> Is this Pseudo-Dionysian hierarchy, or AE-specific? What are the 9 ranks named in this cosmology? Does the "Gambit" describe Lucifer's gamble (his manuscript-and-souls plot to ascend) or the players' gamble (defying him)?

### Chapter 7 — The World Anchor

Major artifact / pivot beat. Possibly the **Orb's true counterpart** — what holds the world together vs what tears the seal. Or possibly **the chrono machine itself** — Lucifer's pause-mechanism. Or something entirely new.

> [!todo] Define the World Anchor
> Is it the Orb? The chrono machine? A separate artifact that protects the seal between Hell and Earth? When/how do players encounter it?

## What needs designing

> *"the players have travelled with [Cicero] from early in the campaign"*
> *"every kilometre [on the train] is a field trial of the new hellfire"*
> *"the bomb is mounted on the train from the previous map"*

But we never specified:
- **How many sessions / what cadence** chapters 2-8 occupy
- **Where geographically** the Orb retrieval takes place (which biomes, regions, stations)
- **What major NPCs** appear besides Cicero and PAVNN
- **What the train route looks like** (is there one continuous track, or does the train jump between stations?)
- **What blocks progress** at each chapter (gates, puzzles, factions to negotiate with)
