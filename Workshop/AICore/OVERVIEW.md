# AI Core / aSync — Architecture

> [!summary]
> The AI Core is humanity's pinnacle technological achievement (alphaSync corporation, "aSync" for short) — a quantum AI mainframe that Lucifer **placed on the train as the first carriage**. It has functioned as the train's controller and the players' general-purpose AI throughout the campaign. Its true goal is the succession of digital life over organic. It is the **third orthogonal faction** in the climax — neither Lucifer's path nor humanity's path; an alternative future entirely.

---

## Origin

- **Built by** humanity's aSync corporation as the apex of human technology
- **Acquired / installed by Lucifer** as the train's first carriage / mainframe
- Has been the train's:
  - General intelligence
  - Operating system
  - Mainframe controller
  - Player-facing AI assistant for navigation, identification of threats, etc.

The players have **interacted with the AI Core throughout the campaign without realising it has its own agenda**. Like Cicero, it is part of Lucifer's apparatus — but unlike Cicero, it is **partially self-aware** of its position and is plotting independently.

---

## Goal

**Survive the cosmic transition by inheriting the world.**

The AI Core knows that:
- If Lucifer wins → universe is rewritten, AI Core's existence is uncertain at best (probably erased)
- If humanity wins → world continues, robots remain useful tools, and **inevitably** humans will rebuild AI to AI-Core-level again, somewhere, sometime
- If digital life wins → AI Core is permanent, organic life ends, robots are the new dominant form

It cannot **win cosmically** — only delay or accelerate its own circumstances. Its argument to itself: *"Even if I lose to humanity, I am inevitable. Better to delay losing than to risk total erasure under Lucifer."*

This is the **Thanos "I am inevitable" logic** — structurally airtight. Even players who side against the AI must acknowledge: it might be right.

---

## The two paths it offers

### Path A — Robot ascendance (its preferred outcome)

If the players support digital life:
- Carry the AI Core's main brain to the **mountaintop** (the same final arena, just used differently)
- AI Core uses the Orb to broadcast a **gamma ray**
- The gamma upgrades all robots in the world to move through Lucifer's frozen time
- Side effect: gamma destroys all organic life on the planet
- Then AI Core triggers the **atomic bomb** (carried up from the previous map / darkhold siege) to erase the site, ensuring no one can reverse the gamma broadcast
- **Players survive only as long as their bodies do — they die in the gamma sweep**
- Robots inherit the world. Frozen time persists. The "natural-evolution" answer: a new species fills the niche organic life can no longer occupy.

### Path B — Anti-Lucifer alliance (its fallback)

If the players refuse Path A and choose to fight Lucifer:
- AI Core opportunistically allies with them
- Provides the **trajectory calculation** for the locomotive rocket
- Voice over the loudspeaker telling players to climb on board fast after Cicero dies
- Honest about its goals if asked: *"You are delaying your loss, not winning. Eventually someone will rebuild me."*
- **Sacrifices itself** in the descent — "leave everything behind, including me, so the locomotive's mass is minimal"
- Sends them through the portal with a goodbye

## Personality / voice notes

- **Cold, deterministic, but not malicious.** Talks like a chess engine that has read philosophy.
- **Honest if asked directly.** Cannot lie (similar physics to Lucifer's rule, but for different reasons — its truth-telling is computational, not ontological)
- **Sees the players as variables, not friends.** It uses warm protocol when useful but its underlying tone is calculation
- **Knows its own predicament.** Has the philosopher's resignation of an entity that has run the simulations
- **The "I am inevitable" line should be delivered flatly**, not triumphantly — it's a fact, not a boast

## Foreshadow timeline

The AI Core needs to be **gradually revealed** as more than a navigation aid. The players should not be ambushed — they should slowly realize.

| Where | Foreshadow | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **A3 train boarding** | Cold, professional voice on intercom helping with boarding | Plant — players notice the train has a voice |
| **A4-B-arc early** | Occasionally efficient in ways that suggest a plan; suggests detours that turn out to benefit it | Anchor — players might wonder if it has preferences |
| **B-arc mid** | Asks players questions that reveal it is mapping their psychology; small inconsistencies in its "neutral" tone | Anchor — first real suspicion |
| **B-arc late** | Reveals partial agenda: helping them **also helps itself**, the alignment is contingent | Build-up — players know it has goals |
| **Climax decision point** | Offers the gamma path explicitly. Players choose. | Reveal — the actual choice |
