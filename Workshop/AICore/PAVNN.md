# PAVNN — companion robot, AI Core operative

> [!summary]
> PAVNN is a digital lifeform / robot companion who has travelled with the players from early in the campaign. He is friendly, useful, beloved. He is also Lucifer-employed, AI-Core-directed, party-helping. His three-layer loyalty chain is: **Lucifer commanded "serve AI Core"; AI Core commands "help the party."** PAVNN executes both without questioning either. In the climax, his sulfur accumulator is the **key that nullifies Lucifer's sustained spell** in Hell, enabling the final boss fight to be winnable. He sacrifices himself.

## Who he is

- A digital lifeform built by aSync (the same corporation that produced the AI Core)

## The loyalty chain

PAVNN does not have an internal conflict — his structure is simple and clean:

1. **Lucifer** commanded him: *"Serve the AI Core."* (Earliest in his timeline.)
2. **AI Core** commanded him: *"Help the party."*
3. **PAVNN** does what he was told: helps the party.

He has free will — he can decide *how* to help, what specific actions, what jokes to make, when to volunteer information. But the foundational *why* (helping the party at all) was decided for him before the campaign began.

This is **Cicero-parallel structure**: both are unconscious servants with free-will execution. The difference is:
- Cicero believes his work is his own ambition (Lucifer's commission disguised as his life's purpose)
- PAVNN believes his orders are his job (Lucifer's commission disguised as employment)

PAVNN is therefore **less to grieve about than Cicero** in one sense (he never thought he was an artist), but **equally tragic** in another (he never had the chance to choose something else).

## The sulfur accumulator

In the climax, PAVNN's role becomes essential:

- His internal accumulator is built around **sulfur** — a substance unique to "this side" (Hell / the otherworld)
- Sulfur **nullifies** Lucifer's sustained spell that protects his true form
- As the accumulator's sulfur acid leaks out, Lucifer's protective incantation **dies**
- Without the spell, Lucifer's true form becomes vulnerable to the quill-as-sword

**PAVNN's sacrifice** is therefore mechanically necessary for the humanity-path final boss fight to be winnable. Without him, the quill is a sword that bounces off.

## Foreshadow timeline

PAVNN's reveal should be **gradual** but earlier than Cicero's, because his payoff is mechanical (he must be present at the boss fight) and players should at least suspect his nature before he sacrifices.

| Where | Anchor | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **B-arc early** | Players see PAVNN respond to AI Core voice with a deference that's *more than protocol* | Plant |
| **B-arc mid** | A combat moment where PAVNN's optimal action would be self-preservation but he chooses to take damage to protect a player | Anchor — he cares, but his behavior is shaped by orders |
| **B-arc late** | A conversation where a player asks "who do you work for?" and PAVNN answers literally (gives the chain), or deflects — depending on player approach | Reveal-adjacent |
| **Hell descent** | PAVNN reveals his sulfur accumulator's purpose en route to the final arena | Setup for sacrifice |
| **Final arena** | Sacrifice + final speech | Payoff |
