# Paused Apocalypse — world state

> [!summary]
> Earth is suspended **mid-double-apocalypse**. Humanity launched their nuclear arsenal; God responded with angels, demons, trumpets, and rapture; before either could finish, **Lucifer stepped onto the fabric of reality with his chrono machine** and pressed pause. The pause did not stop cleanly — some places are frozen mid-scream, others judder forward in fits and starts. The world is "trying to finish dying," and the Apocalypse Express runs through it, the only thing that moves freely.

---

## Canonical history (per `B1 Chapel of Second Chances`)

Lucifer's own retelling, delivered to the players after Revival:

> *"You remember the headlines. Wars, little plagues, big egos. Eventually someone pushed the wrong stack of buttons and lit the sky. Your upstairs neighbour responded with angels and trumpets. Double exposure: man-made suicide, divine eviction."*
>
> *"I did what I could. I stepped out onto the film and pressed pause. It didn't stop cleanly. Some places are frozen mid-scream; others judder forward in fits and starts. That strain you feel in your bones? That's the world trying to finish dying."*

---

## The two apocalypses, in order

**1. Atomic apocalypse (man-made suicide)**

- Long buildup: wars, plagues, big egos
- Final trigger: someone "pushed the wrong stack of buttons and lit the sky"
- Mutual nuclear exchange — global, unrecoverable
- Sky lit with launches, missiles tracking, mushroom clouds beginning to bloom

**2. Divine apocalypse (divine eviction)**

- God's response — initiated as the missiles flew, before they could detonate
- **Earth cracks open** — fissures pour molten rock and **demons** emerge from below
- **Angels descend** from above, trumpets sounding, sky tearing
- **Rapture** begins — souls being lifted out of bodies, taken upward
- This is the "double exposure" — both apocalypses overlap rather than sequence

**3. Lucifer's intervention (the pause)**

- Lucifer "stepped onto the film" — onto the fabric of reality itself
- Activated his **chrono machine** — a device of his own making (Hell-tech / Lucifer-tech)
- **Pressed pause** on time, but the pause did not engage cleanly
- Result: time mostly frozen, but unevenly. Some places fully stilled; others juddering forward in starts and stops. The whole world straining as it tries to complete its cataclysm.

The pause **is not stable**. It is a continuous effort. Lucifer is *holding* it. This is why he cannot leave Hell to retrieve the Orb himself — the moment his attention slips, the pause weakens.

## What the world looks like during the pause

> Per `Sora Example scenes for AE`: *"Everything in the world is perfectly frozen except the train and its steam/smoke."*

The Earth's surface is a **still frame of the apocalypse**. Players walking through B-arc locations encounter:

- **Frozen mushroom clouds** — half-bloomed, fire and smoke suspended outward, fallout particles caught mid-fall
- **Lava mid-pour** from cracked earth, droplets of molten rock hanging in the air like beads
- **Demons** half-emerged from the cracks — claws on the lip, mouths open in soundless roar, wings frozen at extension
- **Angels** mid-descent — light haloed, swords or trumpets in hand, wings caught mid-beat
- **Rapture-people** suspended halfway between earth and sky — feet still touching ground, bodies tilted upward, faces lit from above, possessions falling from open hands
- **Trumpets blasting silently** — the sound never reaching ears
- **Sky tearing** — visible rents in the cloud layer with light or darkness leaking through
- **Buildings** half-collapsing — concrete dust hanging like fog, rebar bent but not yet failed
- **People mid-scream** — caught at the moment of dying or witnessing the dying

Above all of it: a **purple chronon haze** in uneven veils (per Sora guide), the visible signature of the chrono machine's effort.

## "Judder forward in fits and starts" — design implication

Some zones are **not perfectly frozen**. The pause holds unevenly:

- Most places: stilled, walkable, unchanging
- Some places: time **juddering forward** — short bursts of normal flow, then re-freeze, then more bursts
- Some places: time **leaking** at slow continuous rate — a mushroom cloud blooming a millimetre per minute; a demon's claw closing over decades

These **active zones** are where:
- The threats can move (demons advance, lava drips, fire spreads — slowly)
- The puzzles work (the player has to time their actions to the freeze cycle)
- The drama is heaviest (you can hear a partial scream; you can see a hand twitch)
- Reality is **most damaged** — these are the places where the seal is thinnest

> [!todo] Design implication for B-arc encounters
> Most B-arc combat / puzzle locations should be **active zones** — there has to be SOMETHING happening to challenge the players. Pure-frozen zones are good for atmosphere, mystery, and "museum" exploration (per `(Procedure) Rarities`: *"the world became a museum"*).

### Naming convention — The Drift / Drifters

> Sourced from brainstorm canvas (`Assets/Canvas/(Canvas) Ideas brainstorming.canvas`).

- **The Drift** — the active zones where time juddering occurs. Not a single place; a phenomenon distributed across the broken Earth wherever the chrono machine's hold weakens.
- **Drifters** — the beings or people moving in / influenced by the Drift. Possibly:
  - Anomalous creatures created by physics-disruption (time-folded entities)
  - Half-revived humans whose deaths are paused mid-fall and partially reversed
  - Wanderers (player-class?) who have learned to navigate the Drift
  - Hostile entities that emerge specifically in juddering zones

The naming **immediately enters the design vocabulary** — every B-arc scene that touches an active zone references "the Drift," and any anomalous combatant is a "Drifter" of some specific subtype.

## Why the train can move

The Apocalypse Express runs on **new hellfire** — Lucifer's experimental upgraded fuel that can survive cross-realm transit (HALT) and operate in the real world. See [../Climax/OVERVIEW.md → "The new hellfire"](../Climax/OVERVIEW.md#the-new-hellfire--the-train-as-foreshadow).

The train is the **only thing that moves freely** in the paused world (per Sora). It cuts through the frozen tableau like a needle through a still photograph. Its steam and smoke are the only motion in many vistas.

This is also why the players can move — they were brought back from Hell **on the train**, in printed bodies that operate on the same hellfire principle. They are, in effect, **proxies of the train's own animation**. The Pact in A5 essentially licenses them to share in the train's exemption from the freeze.

## The "strain in the bones"

Per Lucifer in B1: *"That strain you feel in your bones? That's the world trying to finish dying."*

This is a continuous low-level sensory cue the players should feel throughout the B-arc:

- A pressure behind the eyes
- An ache in the joints
- A sense that gravity is *almost* misaligned
- Sudden moments of vertigo when looking too long at a frozen demon's face
- Sounds that don't quite track (a frozen scream half-heard, like a memory bleeding through)

This is **the cosmic background radiation of a paused apocalypse**. Constant. Unfixable. Reminds the players every moment that this is **not** their normal world — it is a held breath.
