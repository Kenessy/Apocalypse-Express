# StoryFlow Opening Mini-Sync

> Prototype-facing sync note for the StoryFlow Editor / HTML visual novel direction. This is a Workshop note, not a replacement for the tabletop Database scenes.

---

## Current prototype direction

- Tooling: StoryFlow Editor.
- Target format: single-player visual novel / light gameplay HTML game.
- Current build state: main menu is already in.
- Next focus: first playable story flow, not complex UI or systems.

---

## Opening scene

The protagonist wakes at an obsidian fountain / obsidian platform.

The protagonist does not know:

- where they are,
- why they are there,
- what happened,
- why they specifically woke there.

---

## Location meaning

The obsidian platform is part of Lucifer's plan / construction.

It is Hell-linked, but not a simple burning-Hell scene. It is a liminal gathering space where souls and fragments of worlds, dirt, objects, and layers of reality have drifted together.

The place should feel like a constructed spiritual junction: not a normal afterlife room, not a normal ruin, not a normal station yet.

---

## Story function

This is the tutorial / opening mystery space.

The player's first job is to:

1. wake up,
2. orient themselves,
3. look around,
4. test the space,
5. experience the first anomaly,
6. make first contact,
7. begin realizing that Lucifer gathered them here for a purpose.

---

## Probable first playable flow

```text
Main Menu
  -> Obsidian Fountain Awakening
  -> Look Around / Call Out / Touch Fountain
  -> First Anomaly
  -> First Contact
  -> Lucifer's offer / pressure / reveal
  -> Boarding path toward the Express
```

## Design note

Keep the opening small and playable. The first goal is not to explain the entire cosmology. The first goal is to make the player understand one thing through interaction:

> This place was built to gather them, and someone expected them to wake here.
