# Factions — top-level overview

Quick map of who is who, what they want, and what they secretly serve. The campaign's moral geometry is **three orthogonal forces** competing for the future of reality, plus the players who must align with one (or fall by default into Lucifer's path).

---

## The three orthogonal forces

The strategic space of the campaign is **exhausted** by three forces. Every player choice — including doing nothing — falls into one of these three buckets. There is no "abstention." Free will, in this cosmology, has no off-switch.

### 1. Humanity / organic life

- **Wants:** survival of organic life, continuation of the world as-is (with whatever damage repaired)
- **Strategic posture:** defensive — trying to preserve what exists
- **Climax objective:** kill Lucifer's true form (requires Phase 2 / Hell descent)
- **Ending if they win:** *Continue ending — soft restart.* World survives, life goes on, scars heal slowly.

### 2. Lucifer / celestial rebellion

- **Wants:** burn this universe, recreate a new one in his image (using souls as fuel + Cicero's manuscript as template + the Orb as seal-breaker)
- **Strategic posture:** apocalyptic — destruction as creation
- **Climax objective:** activate the Orb on the broken bridge, initiate the new hellfire, take the manuscript to Hell
- **Ending if he wins:** *Hard restart / recreation.* Universe ends, new universe is generated, Lucifer is its architect

### 3. AI Core / aSync / digital lifeforms

- **Wants:** survival of digital life, succession of robots as the dominant form. Will not "win" cosmically — only delays its own loss
- **Strategic posture:** opportunistic — picks the best position for long-term survival
- **Climax objective:** broadcast gamma ray from the mountaintop using the Orb → upgrades all robots to move through frozen time, kills all organic life. Then nuke the area to prevent reversal.
- **Ending if they win:** *Limbo ending.* Frozen time persists, organic life ends, robots inherit the world. Doesn't solve the cosmic problem — sidesteps it (natural-selection logic: if one species can't inhabit the niche, another comes that can)

## Key actors per faction

### Humanity / organic life
- **The players** (the only true free-will agents in the cosmology — see below)
- NPCs, allied human forces from earlier maps, the resistance
- *(Cicero is technically organic-aligned in form, but is structurally Lucifer's apparatus — see below)*

### Lucifer / celestial
- **Lucifer himself** — wears an earthly avatar throughout; his true form is in Hell, only vulnerable there
- **God** — present only by absence. Designed the system that emergent-produced Lucifer. May or may not respond to anything.
- **Cicero** — *appears* as warm chronicler companion. Is actually Lucifer's commissioned manuscript-writer. Doesn't know the true purpose of his work. See [Cicero/OVERVIEW.md](Cicero/OVERVIEW.md).

### AI Core / digital
- **AI Core itself** — quantum AI mainframe, was the train's first carriage all along; humanity's aSync corporation built it; Lucifer parked it on the train as part of his apparatus
- **PAVNN** — digital lifeform companion the players have travelled with. Lucifer-employed → AI-Core-directed → party-helping. Three-layer execution chain. See [AICore/PAVNN.md](AICore/PAVNN.md).
- Other robot NPCs encountered through the campaign

## The free will substrate

**Critical cosmological note:** the players are the **only true free-will agents** in this universe. Lucifer is an emergent feature of the free-will substrate God designed; Cicero and PAVNN are commissioned servants who execute commands; the AI Core acts on its own deterministic logic. **Only the players are not pre-determined.**

This means:
- The players' choices are **not** pre-written in Cicero's book (the book records events as they happen, but the players' decisions are the source of those events)
- The players are the **only variable** in the cosmic equation
- Their choice between the three endings is **the** cosmologically meaningful decision

This is also why **defiance is canonical**: defiance is the act of free will most thoroughly demonstrating its own existence. The other two endings (robot, Lucifer) are players choosing to align with deterministic systems. The humanity ending is players choosing themselves.

## Layered loyalty — Lucifer as the patron of patrons

Lucifer set up **everything** as his apparatus, even the appearance of opposition:

| Actor | Direct master | Aware of being commissioned? |
|---|---|---|
| Cicero | Lucifer | No (believes his work is for posterity) |
| AI Core | Lucifer | Partial (knows its origins, doesn't know all of Lucifer's final use) |
| PAVNN | AI Core (which is Lucifer's) | No (just follows orders) |
| The Train | Lucifer | Unknown / possibly conscious — see climax |
| Players | None | n/a — free will |

The reveal arc gradually exposes how many things were Lucifer's apparatus all along.
