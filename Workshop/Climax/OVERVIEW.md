# Climax — full architecture

> [!summary]
> The campaign converges on a **single arena** (mountaintop + broken bridge + frozen train mid-air) with **three orthogonal endings** the players can choose from. The arena's exact configuration is **pre-locked by player choices throughout the campaign**, so by the time they arrive, much of the setup is already determined. The choice they make AT the arena determines which of three endings plays out.

## Stage

**Location:** mountaintop at the end of the world. A broken bridge extends out into open air. The train is frozen in mid-air, mid-leap, on Lucifer's timestop. Lucifer is also frozen in the scene. The party arrives, the parley begins.

**What's on board the train:**
- The locomotive (the original, "the train's soul" — picked them up in A3)
- Multiple carriages earned through the campaign: armory, medbay, storage, etc.
- The atomic bomb mounted from the previous map (darkhold siege)
- Cicero, his book, his quill
- PAVNN
- The AI Core (still on board as the train's first carriage / mainframe)
- Whatever loot, NPCs, equipment the players have accumulated

**Setup pre-locked by player choices throughout campaign:**
- Allies present at the arena (human resistance forces? remaining robots? neutral parties?)
- Which carriages were upgraded, lost, repurposed
- Player relationships with Cicero, PAVNN, AI Core, key NPCs
- Whether players know the avatar/true-form distinction for Lucifer
- Whether players have figured out the AI Core's agenda

## Phase 1 — common to all paths

The mid-air parley is the same for all three endings up to the choice point.

1. **Arrival** — train leaps the broken bridge, freezes mid-air. Lucifer is waiting on the bridge (or in the air alongside).
2. **Parley** — Lucifer makes his pitch. He explains what the Orb does, what he plans, why it has to be him.
3. **Cicero present** — recording everything. PAVNN present. AI Core voice on intercom.
4. **The AI Core makes its offer (or has already, depending on prior reveals).** This is where the **branch decision** happens — players signal which path they're taking.

## Phase 2A — Humanity path (canonical "defiance")

The hardest path, the one with the largest content footprint.

5. **Defiance.** Players refuse Lucifer. He shrugs, takes the Orb himself.
6. **Orb activation.** The Orb absorbs Lucifer's energy — but since he is otherworldly, the energy flows from Hell INTO him. God's seal between Hell and Earth breaks (overridden by God's own Orb).
7. **The new hellfire emerges.** Purple firestorm bursts from the Orb, incinerates the Orb itself, then spreads slowly outward, consuming the world. Lucifer reveals: this is a NEW invention, the players have been unwittingly using it as "hellfire" all campaign.
8. **Lucifer's monologue.** [Full text in LuciferMonologue.md.] Delivered AFTER activation, while the firestorm spreads. Addresses God directly. Receives no response — silence.
9. **Cicero strikes.** As the monologue ends, Cicero approaches Lucifer with the quill and stabs him. Lucifer laughs.
10. **Lucifer self-immolates.** *"Okay, then I die"* — walks into his own fire. The fireproof being is incinerated in seconds because he chooses it. The book vanishes with him in a flash — the avatar and the manuscript leave together as one unit, transferring to Hell.
11. **Cicero dies.** Players gather around him. He hands them the quill and tells them to descend, to finish the cycle where they started. *"Csak ott fejezhetik be ahol elkezdték az egészet."* He drops a hint about the train.
12. **The locomotive puzzle.**
    - Detach all carriages — sacrifice the campaign's accumulated assets
    - Collect the new hellfire as fuel for the locomotive
    - Watch the carriages burn / fall / freeze in the abandoned air
    - Load the locomotive with the firestorm material
13. **AI Core farewell.** Voice over intercom delivers the trajectory calculations. "Leave everything behind including me, so the mass is minimal." Goodbye line. AI Core is left behind with the carriages.
14. **Locomotive launch.** Players climb on board the bare locomotive. Unfreeze. Ballistic trajectory through the broken bridge into the spreading Hell portal.
15. **Descent.** The locomotive — itself an otherworldly creature — begins to melt in the firestorm. Windows soften, shake "like a living thing — sweating from the inside, perhaps for the first time." It dies giving them safe passage.
16. **Crash into Hell.** Final arena: the deepest part of Hell.
17. **PAVNN sacrifice.** PAVNN reveals his sulfur accumulator's purpose. Sulfur is unique to this side; it nullifies Lucifer's sustained spell that protects his true form. As the accumulator leaks, the spell dies.
18. **PAVNN's final speech.** [Stub in PAVNN.md.] He explains what he was, why, and that he chose to care along the way.
19. **Final boss fight.** Lucifer's true form. Fire aura, DOT fire damage, spirit damage. Pure boss combat. **No more help** — back to the original core party only. The quill becomes a sword in the player's hand who wields it (otherworld physics: form follows need).
20. **Cosmic outcome.** If players defeat Lucifer's true form: Continue ending — soft restart. The new hellfire stops spreading. The world begins to heal. The seal repairs over time.

## Phase 2B — Robot path (AI ascendance)

5. **AI alignment.** Players signal they support the AI's plan. They protect or assist the AI Core's brain being carried up.
6. **Setup at the mountaintop.** The Orb gets to the AI's broadcast position. The atomic bomb is positioned for post-broadcast erasure.
7. **Lucifer reacts.** The avatar is hostile to this plan — players fight Lucifer's avatar to delay him while the AI works.
8. **Boss fight (delay variant).** Survive long enough for the AI to complete the gamma broadcast. *(See QUESTIONS — is the avatar killable here?)*
9. **Gamma broadcast.** Massive radiation pulse from the Orb. All robots in the world get the upgrade to move through frozen time. All organic life on the planet dies in proximity, including the players.
10. **Atomic erasure.** AI triggers the bomb. Site is erased. Nothing remains that could reverse the broadcast.
11. **Limbo ending.** Frozen time persists. Robots inherit the world. The cosmic problem is sidestepped, not solved.

## Phase 2C — Lucifer path (compliance)

5. **Compliance.** Players hand over the Orb willingly. (Or remain passive while Lucifer takes it — equivalent.)
6. **Activation.** Same as Path A step 6 — but uninterrupted by Cicero.
7. **The new hellfire spreads.** Players watch.
8. **Lucifer's monologue** — possibly different version (less defensive, more triumphant) since the players didn't resist.
9. **Cicero records faithfully.** Probably does not strike. Death by firestorm shortly after, alongside the players.
10. **Hard restart.** Universe ends. New universe begins, generated from Cicero's manuscript. Lucifer is its architect.

## The new hellfire — the train as foreshadow

> [!important] The Apocalypse Express **itself** is the unwitting tool. Not an item, not an ability — the **vehicle**. Players have been *riding* the world-burner from A3 onward.

### Canonical baseline (per `Database/20_Rules/(Rule) 6. Currencies in A.E.`)

- *"Hellfire burns souls."* In standard AE, hellfire is the substance produced when souls are processed — it **purifies** them (transition / passing) and yields **portable obedient energy**.
- **Sparkplugs** are canisters of this energy — *"distilled from what used to be someone."* Rare, taboo, beyond price.
- Sparkplugs power **Hellfire boilers**, including the one that "shoves a steam train through purple haze."

### Lucifer's upgrade — the new hellfire

Lucifer experimentally developed an **upgraded** hellfire ash:

- Doesn't just **purify** — actively **burns / consumes** souls (terminal; no continuation; complete erasure)
- **Higher energy yield**
- **Can be brought up to the real world** — previously hellfire couldn't survive transit out of Hell
- Powers the Apocalypse Express boiler **specifically** — the AE is the first vehicle ever to traverse the HALT and operate in the real world. **The new hellfire is what makes the train possible.**

### The unwitting use

From A3 onward, the players board the AE and **ride it through the entire B-arc**. Every kilometre is powered by new hellfire. Every Sparkplug they spend on Revival, every overclock, every train operation — running on Lucifer's experimental fuel.

**They cannot opt out.** The vehicle is the use. The reveal at the climax is therefore not "you used a tool you shouldn't have" but "you ARE the test subjects. The whole journey was the trial run for the substance that will now burn your world."

### Climax reveal — Lucifer's specific line

When Lucifer activates the Orb and the firestorm spreads, his reveal can land like:

> *"Tudtátok mi hajtotta a vonatotokat? Hellfire — mondtátok, miközben aláírtátok a Pact-et. Új hellfire — mondom most, miközben felgyújtom vele a világot. Az Apocalypse Express volt az első prototípus ami túlélte a HALT-tranzitot. Minden kilométer egy mező-teszt volt. Köszönöm hogy lefuttattátok az utolsó próbát helyettem."*

Or English variant:

> *"You knew what powered your train. Hellfire, you said, when you signed. New hellfire, I say now, while I light the world with it. The Apocalypse Express was the first prototype to survive the HALT. Every kilometre you rode was a field trial. Thank you for completing the last one for me."*

### Implications elsewhere

- **A5 Sundown Saloon** — Lucifer's pact pitch can include a casual reference to the train's fuel. *"You'll find the train runs on hellfire — quite efficient. Don't think about it too much."* Truthful (it's hellfire) but evades the new-vs-old distinction.
- **A3-A4 train operations** — any time the boiler is mentioned, players hear "hellfire" or "Sparkplug" — never "new hellfire." The labeling consistency IS the deception.
- **B-arc combat / Revival** — every time a Sparkplug is consumed, players are spending new hellfire. Foreshadow is automatic.
- **The locomotive descent (Phase 2A)** — the locomotive's inability to fully resist the firestorm in the descent now makes sense in a new way: it is **made of** the same substance. The firestorm consumes its own vehicle. The "sweating from inside" beat is the locomotive *recognising* what's burning it.
