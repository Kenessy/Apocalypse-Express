# Endings Table

Three winning endings, all converging at the same physical arena (mountaintop + broken bridge + frozen mid-air train), branching by player choice.

> "Refuse all" is **not an option** — the three factions exhaust the strategic space. Even passivity falls into Lucifer's path (the firestorm starts without input). The cosmology has no off-switch for free will.

## Decision triggers

> [!todo] Lock the decision mechanic per path

How does the GM know which path the players are committing to? Some thoughts:

- **Humanity = active resistance.** Refuse Lucifer, attack the avatar, attempt to disrupt the Orb. Defaulted to if players actively oppose.
- **Robots = active assistance to AI Core.** Carry the brain core, protect the broadcast, fight Lucifer to delay. Requires explicit player commitment to the AI's plan.
- **Lucifer = passive or active compliance.** Hand over the Orb voluntarily, OR simply do nothing while Lucifer takes it. Default fallback if players pick neither active path.

A clear "now choose" beat in the parley scene helps the GM commit. The AI Core's pitch + Lucifer's pitch + the players' line of dialogue or action triggers the lock.

## Pre-locked elements (set before the climax)

Many of the climax's specific configurations are determined by player choices throughout the campaign. By the time players arrive at the arena, much is already in place:

| Element | Determined by |
|---|---|
| Allies present at the arena | Whether players helped/abandoned key NPCs through the campaign |
| Carriage configuration | Which carriages were upgraded, lost, sacrificed |
| Player relationships with Cicero, PAVNN, AI Core | Conversation / dialogue choices through B-arc |
| Whether players have figured out avatar/true-form | Whether they investigated the right clues |
| Whether players have figured out AI Core's agenda | Whether they questioned PAVNN, AI Core, certain NPCs |
| Player health/equipment going in | All campaign decisions |
| Atomic bomb status | Whether they kept it from the darkhold map intact |

These pre-locks mean **the same arena plays differently** depending on what the players brought to it. The arena is a stage; the decisions made onstage are what differ.

## Hidden / future paths (not yet canonical)

### Fates / book-repair branch (humanity-path variant)

If the players, in Phase 2A Hell descent, recover Cicero's book from Lucifer (or copy it, or use the quill to "write back" something), they may be able to use it to **repair tears in the fabric of reality** caused by the seal-breaking. This would be a hidden 4th path — humanity wins AND the long-term cycle is broken.

Backlog at: future `Workshop/Weavers/`. Do not commit to canon yet.

### Lucifer-betrayal at the throne (humanity-path variant)

After delivering Lucifer the Orb on the Lucifer-path, players may have one last chance to betray him before the new universe completes. Difficult, low success rate, but possible.

Backlog. Possibly redundant with the Humanity path.
